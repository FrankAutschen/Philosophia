# One. Confucian Social Ontology

1.The meaning of Social Ontology in Chinese Philisophy
- Benti=the thing iteself(not ding an sich),the actual,physcical object; Ontology=logic theory of things
- Basic Assumption: static and essentialist "being" *vs.* active and non-essentialist "becoming";basic paradigms of Confucian thought-relationality,creativity,vitality and process

# Two.  Problem of the  Relation Self

1.Two theories on the relational self
- Relational Adhesion
- Relational Constitution

2.The individual,encumbered, and transscendent self
- self is individual,autonomous and rational.
- the society is encumbered,like Rousseau.

3.The paradox of subjectivation
- In theories of subjectivation, the self is a paradoxical product of power relations, as discussed by Althusser, Foucault, and Butler.
- In social constructionist theory, the self is a collectively shaped dynamic process, as articulated by Dewey, Mead, and Vygotsky.
