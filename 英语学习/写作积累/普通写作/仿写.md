
1.这正是许多 X 所欣赏或遗憾的事情
>原文：This is precisely the sort of thing many of Palantir’s British critics deplore.
>句式：This is precisely the sort of thing many of X appreciate/deplore.

2.很难想象还有比执行Z能更好设计实现Y的X组合
>原文：It is hard to think of a combination of policies better designed to raise prices than to place new taxes on imports and then give people free money with which to buy them.
>句式：It is hard to think of a combination of X better designed to do Y  than to do Z.

3.所有这一切都发生在一个 X 时代
>原文：All of this is taking place in an era of....
>句式：All of this is taking place in an era of X.
