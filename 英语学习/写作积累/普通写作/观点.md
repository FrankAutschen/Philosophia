##  一、经济

1. 发展本土企业不能依靠贸易保护

>In a world of **geopolitical fracture**, governments may sometimes want **homegrown alternatives** to foreign technology.The best way to nurture these is to give **domestic incumbents** and startups an incentive to innovate. **Cosseting** local firms by **blocking foreign competition** has the opposite effect.
